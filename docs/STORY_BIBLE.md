# Sky Archer — Story Bible

16 Oct 2026 · Dan

## Purpose of this document
This is the backstory behind Sky Archer. The player never receives it as one telling; it is revealed in small fragments across the rifts.

Its job is to drive design decisions with intention. It is not meant to be complete or to explain everything yet.

## The world before
The world was home to four peoples: wizards, clerics, warriors and archers. They lived in harmony and cooperation, practicing their fights and crafts in this dimension.

Wizards and archers were friends. The wizards made magical items for the archers. These items are why archers could compete with wizards at all.

Elements are tied directly to wizards. All elemental magic in the world comes from them.

Gods exist in this world. Their place in the lore is not yet defined.

## The rise of the cult
A wizard rose who, through wisdom and dedication, became the strongest wizard ever to live. He was the protagonist's childhood best friend.

**His turn.** He was not born evil. He turned after discovering a new kind of magic: dark portal magic. His views trace back to how he was raised, and he holds those he sees as "weak bloods" in contempt.

**The cult.** He recruited followers and taught them his ways, destroying the balance and spreading chaos and damage everywhere. This became the Cult of the Wicked. Eventually every living wizard had either been killed by the cult or joined it.

**The fall of the archers.** The archers were the only ones strong enough to challenge the wizards. The wizards took back the magical items they had made for them, confiscating and locking them away, and the archers were wiped out. One survived: you. You had sided with him, because you were best friends.

**The others.**
- **Clerics** isolated themselves in their monasteries, practicing healing and worship. The wizards respected them, since clerics also dabbled in magic.
- **Warriors** stayed with the townsfolk, women and children, protecting them. The wizards, smug in their supremacy, left them alone, knowing warriors were helpless against them.

## The rupture
Portal magic ultimately destroyed the dimension as its people knew it. Two portals collided, sending a massive ripple across reality and tearing it apart.

- Many of the wizards were wiped out in the rupture.
- The evil wizard isolated himself in his tower. He has the power to restore the world but refuses to accept responsibility and repair it. He would rather see the world shattered than have all the weak bloods back in it.
- The spirit of the world was released by the rupture and manifested a form.
- You lost part of your memory, including the fact that you sided with him.

## The fractured world
What remains is floating islands above an endless void, a world divided by portals.

**Lost artifacts.** Many artifacts are scattered across the world: the bows and quivers the wizards confiscated from the archers, and the magical rings the wizards forged.

**Creatures.** The rupture reshaped life in three ways:
- **Extinction.** Many creatures died out. Very few survived the new environment, the bird among them.
- **Mutation.** Some existing creatures changed, such as apples growing wings.
- **Arrivals.** Creatures from other dimensions now spawn into this world.

Some of these creatures are aggressive towards you; others are helpful.

## The protagonist and the hidden past
You are the last archer, trying to reach the evil wizard in his tower. The rupture took part of your memory, and you piece together the story, along with friends and family you had, as you move through the rifts.

Your past holds two secrets, and each can only surface in certain ways:

| Secret | Who knows | How it surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| You sided with the evil wizard | Some of your fallen companions; others don't | Accusers you meet along the way. You don't believe them at first, but you eventually figure it out. |
| He was your childhood best friend | Only you and him | Your returning memory, or him. Revealed at the end. |

**Why did you survive?** Partly because you sided with him. The full reason is not yet decided, and memory loss leaves room to settle it later.

## Characters

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| The evil wizard | Leader of the Cult of the Wicked, your childhood best friend. Hides in his tower and refuses to repair the world. |
| The spirit of the world | Your main guide in the hub; wants to help you repair the world. An essence, not a conscious being: it doesn't interact with creatures the way they interact with each other, so it doesn't know your past. |
| The defector wizard | A wizard who defied the cult and survived. |
| Surviving clerics | Want to help you. |
| Sky pirates | Raid the skies of the void seeking gold, trading artifacts they found in exchange. |
| Spirits of fallen companions | Companions you had. Some know you sided with the wizard, others don't. |
| Accusers | People met along the way who accuse you of siding with the wizard. You don't believe them. |

**The spirit's discovery.** The spirit only learns what you did after you've already done a lot to help it. It spares you, carrying a message about actions.

## Story in the mechanics

| Mechanic | In the fiction |
|---|---|
| Falling into the void | There is nothing below, so the void spits you back into the world, damaging you in the process. |
| Dying | Once you are too weak to resist the void's power, you are sent back to the safe haven (the hub). |
| Items reset on death | You become too weak to hold them (low priority). |
| Bows and quivers | Magical items the wizards made for the archers, later confiscated and now lost around the world. |
| Rings | Magical rings forged by the wizards, lost around the world. |
| Elements | Tied directly to wizards. |
| Rifts and portals | The world is divided by portals after the rupture. |
| Enemies | Survivors, mutated creatures, and arrivals from other dimensions. Under consideration: wizards in the rifts who open portals to summon enemies against you. |
| Bosses | Wizard bosses. |
| Merchant | Sky pirates trading found artifacts for gold. |

## The ending
The goal is to bring the evil wizard down and force him to repair the world. At the end, it comes out that he was your childhood best friend.

Possible outcomes of the confrontation (not yet chosen):
- Right before he dies, he portals out.
- He casts something that breaks the world further instead.

## Open questions
- Did portal magic corrupt him, or only give an ambitious person the power to act? This decides whether the rest of the cult are villains or victims.
- Where did you, an archer, sit in his "weak bloods" view?
- Which ending outcome, and what is the final resolution if he keeps escaping?
- How involved were you in the confiscations and the rupture?
- What is the full reason you survived?
- Where do the gods fit in the lore?
