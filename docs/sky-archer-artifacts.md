# Sky Archer — Artifacts

24 lore artifacts in 8 batches of three, found in the rifts and brought back to the world spirit. Artifacts are fully separate from equipment: lore only, no gameplay effect.

- A batch spawns once the previous batch is fully collected. Order within a batch is random, so each batch works in any order.
- **Spirit** lines play when an artifact is brought back. **After** lines play on revisit, once the spirit's discovery (after batch 7) has happened, and only where the meaning changes.
- Batch 8 spawns in the tower, not at random.

---

## Batch 1: The Aftermath

### House Key
**In the world:** Still in the lock of a splintered door slab lying flat in the grass. The player pulls it out.

**Context:** An iron house key on a cord, a name scratched into its bow. The rupture took ordinary homes. The name can belong to a companion spirit met later.

**Spirit:** "It opens a door that isn't anywhere anymore. Someone carried it anyway, as if they might go home."

### Frozen Hourglass
**In the world:** Hanging motionless in midair above an island, the sand suspended mid-fall, a faint shimmer around it.

**Context:** Stopped at the moment of the rupture. Without the spirit realizing, it's the first acknowledgement of your amnesia.

**Spirit:** "This stopped when I broke. Everything before it is memory now. Some of those memories are yours."

**After:** "I said some memories were yours. I didn't know which ones."

### Warrior's Shield
**In the world:** Leaning against a small cairn of stones, a warrior's grave marker.

**Context:** A battered round shield, its inside face painted with small children's handprints. The warriors' dignity, set against the wizards' "weak bloods" contempt.

**Spirit:** "The wizards thought these ones were helpless, so they ignored them. They protected everything the wizards ignored."

## Batch 2: Your People

### Confiscation Ledger
**In the world:** Inside a cracked cult lockbox, its lid split open.

**Context:** A leather book with the cult seal and columns for item, owner and vault. Every line is ticked except one: "retained by request." That line is your bow.

**Spirit:** "A list of what was stolen from your people. We can take them back."

**After:** "A list of what was taken. Not from you."

### Hearthstone
**In the world:** In the ash of a long-cold campfire ring. The only warm thing there, a faint heat shimmer above it.

**Context:** A smooth river stone that never cools. Archer families kept one per home. This one is your family's, left unconfirmed. It's warmer in the player's hand than anywhere else.

**Spirit:** "This one remembers a home. It's warmer with you. Does it remember yours?"

### Spiral Buckle
**In the world:** Still attached to a torn quiver strap snagged on a dead branch at an island's edge. The player unhooks it.

**Context:** A brass buckle with a small spiral engraved near the pin. The first sighting of his maker's mark.

**Spirit:** "A maker leaves a mark the way a river leaves a bend. I don't know this maker yet."

## Batch 3: The Others and the Gods

### Monastery Bell
**In the world:** Hanging from a frayed rope on a leaning wooden post, swaying in the wind without a sound.

**Context:** A small cracked bronze handbell that rang once during the rupture and never again. Establishes the clerics as credible witnesses for batch 5.

**Spirit:** "The clerics still pray. They ask whether anyone answers. I don't know who they are asking."

### Unanswered Offering
**In the world:** On a small roadside stone shrine, the fruit inside still fresh.

**Context:** A stone altar bowl whose offering has never rotted. A placeholder for the gods; works whether they withdrew, died, or are watching.

**Spirit:** "Someone gave this to the gods. Nothing has come to take it. Nothing has answered anything since the break."

### Zeus Plaque
**In the world:** Bolted to a broken weapon rack half-buried in rubble. The player pries it off.

**Context:** A workshop dedication plaque honouring Zeus, the spiral in the corner. Wizards named their finest works after gods, and he made the Zeus bow.

**Spirit:** "This mark again. Whoever this was made many things for archers. A friend to your people, surely."

**After:** "A friend to your people. I was wrong about who they were a friend to."

## Batch 4: The Wicked

### Defector's Staff
**In the world:** Driven upright into the ground like a warning, a cult edict nailed to it fluttering in the wind.

**Context:** Snapped and scorched. The edict names the defector as the one who opened the rift. Cult propaganda that sets up a false antagonist.

**Spirit:** "Then this is the one we must stop."

**After meeting the defector:** "The paper lied. I believed it because it was written down."

### Initiate's Mask
**In the world:** Lying face-down in the grass. The tally marks only show once it's picked up.

**Context:** A plain cult mask with rows of tallies scratched inside the brim. The initiate was counting days until they planned to leave. Plants the idea that cultists are victims.

**Spirit:** "Someone counted something, many times. Creatures count what they're waiting for."

### Two-Needle Compass
**In the world:** Floating and slowly spinning in place. The case turns, but both needles stay fixed in opposite directions.

**Context:** Explains what the rupture was, two portals colliding, but not who caused it.

**Spirit:** "Two doors opened in the same place. Nothing could stand where they met. Not even me."

## Batch 5: The Accusations

### Wrong Accusation
**In the world:** Nailed to a wooden post like a wanted notice.

**Context:** Signed testimony claiming you led a raid on a monastery, with a date and specific details. Genuinely false, which gives the player permission to dismiss every accuser.

**Spirit:** "The clerics say no archer ever came to them. This one lies."

### Unsent Letter
**In the world:** Tucked inside a satchel slumped open against some rocks.

**Context:** From a companion, never sealed: "They say things about you. I don't believe them. Come home before the muster." Their faith was misplaced. Sets up the Muster Roll.

**Spirit:** "It was never sent. Creatures keep the words they mean most."

**After:** "They believed you. So did I."

### Fletching in a Fallen Archer
**In the world:** Lodged in moss-covered archer remains slumped against a rock. The player draws it out.

**Context:** An arrow with distinctive three-colour fletching. Your fletching style.

**Spirit:** "An archer's arrow inside an archer. The wicked used your own weapons against you."

**After:** "Your arrows. In your people."

**Event after batch 5:** you meet the defector alive, exposing the Staff as propaganda.

## Batch 6: Who He Was

### "On Thin Blood"
**In the world:** A charred book resting on a lectern stump. Only a few pages survive, and the player takes those.

**Context:** His treatise, calm and academic. One page exempts "certain archers" without naming them. Archers sit beneath him, with one exception.

**Spirit:** "He writes as if the world were a garden and most of it weeds."

**After:** "Certain archers. One."

### Scratched Portrait
**In the world:** Face-down in the rubble of a collapsed wall, its frame cracked.

**Context:** A wizard household portrait with the father's face scraped away and a boy at the front. Shows his upbringing; the boy is him.

**Spirit:** "Creatures carve away what hurt them. I don't understand it, but I've seen it often."

### Split Practice Target
**In the world:** Both halves leaning together against a stake at an island's edge.

**Context:** One half scorched, the other full of arrow holes. Two children practiced here together.

**Spirit:** "An archer and a wizard fought here. Fire against arrows. Did the archer win?"

**After:** "No one fought here. Did they?"

## Batch 7: The Net Closes

### Safe-Passage Seal
**In the world:** Hanging from a ribbon tied to a cult waypoint lantern, swaying.

**Context:** A cult wax seal granting free movement through wizard lands. It's yours.

**Spirit:** "The wicked gave these to those they wanted to spare. Or to those they wanted to follow."

**After:** "To those they wanted to spare. You."

### Muster Roll
**In the world:** Inside a dented scroll case wedged between rocks beneath a torn archer banner.

**Context:** The roll for the archers' last stand. Your name is struck through in a hand that isn't the captain's. Whether you left or someone removed you to protect you is deliberately unresolved; it feeds the question of why you survived.

**Spirit:** "Your name was here, and then someone decided it shouldn't be."

### Pirate Appraisal Slip
**In the world:** Inside a small drop pouch tied to a deflated pirate balloon snagged on an island.

**Context:** A trade note valuing a bow far above market, noting the seller paid in cult gold to have it "found by the right hands." He's steering items to you.

**Spirit:** "Someone paid the pirates to make sure this reached you. They paid in the wicked's gold."

**After:** "He wanted you armed. Even now."

**Event after batch 7:** the spirit's discovery. Unlocks every After line.

## Batch 8: The Tower

### First Portal Shard
**In the world:** Floating in the air and humming, a sliver of another sky visible inside, small debris slowly orbiting it.

**Context:** Where his turn began. Whether it whispers decides whether he was corrupted or chose everything himself.

**Spirit:** "This is where I began to break."

### Hollow Ring
**In the world:** Hanging on its chain from a hook beside an empty vault shelf.

**Context:** A beginner's ring with an empty setting. It's yours: the first ring he ever forged, a gift when you were children. He took it back with the confiscations but couldn't bring himself to vault it. He wears its twin.

**Spirit:** "It holds nothing. Why would anyone keep a failure this long?"

### Whittled Arrow
**In the world:** Resting on a tower windowsill, pointing out at the void.

**Context:** A child's unbalanced toy arrow with two names, one gouged out by a later, careful hand, and a small wobbly spiral near the tip. The reveal: the player connects the spiral to him as a child, and the gouged name is yours.

**Spirit:** "The mark. Smaller, clumsier. A child made this."
